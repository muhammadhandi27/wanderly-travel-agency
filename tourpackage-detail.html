Japan, One City at a Time

Tokyo & Kyoto, Japan

7 Days / 6 Nights ★★★★★ Rating 4.9 dari 5 4.9 (128 reviews)

Overview

A combination of two cities with contrasting paces: starting in bustling Tokyo and moving on to Kyoto for a more tranquil atmosphere. The itinerary is designed so you don't have to change cities every day, ensuring you have ample time to explore.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Tokyo

    Upon arrival at Narita Airport, transfer to the hotel in the Shinjuku area. The rest of the day is for resting.

  2. Day 2

    Central Tokyo

    Visit Shibuya, Harajuku, and Meiji Shrine. The evening is free for independent exploration.

  3. Day 3

    Asakusa & Tokyo Skytree

    Visit Sensoji Temple in Asakusa, followed by the Tokyo Skytree area in the afternoon.

  4. Day 4

    Travel to Kyoto

    Travel to Kyoto via Shinkansen. Check in at the hotel, followed by a leisurely walk in the Gion area.

  5. Day 5

    Temples of Kyoto

    Visiting Fushimi Inari Shrine and Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion).

  6. Day 6

    Arashiyama

    Visit the Bamboo Grove and the area around the Katsura River. The evening is free for souvenir shopping.

  7. Day 7

    Departure

    Transfer to Kansai Airport for the return flight.
