ABOUT WANDERLY

We Help Make Travel Easier to Plan.

Our Story

Planning a trip can be fun, but seeking information from too many sources often makes the process exhausting.

Wanderly was created to help simplify that part.

We have gathered a selection of destinations, itineraries, and travel experiences in one place to make it easier for you to make a decision.

WHAT WE VALUE

How We Approach Travel Planning

Good Recommendations

We prefer to provide several relevant options rather than create a list that is too long.

Clear Information

Price, duration, location, and trip details should be easy to find before you make a decision.

Flexible Experiences

An itinerary is a guide, not a set of unchangeable rules.

Careers

Wanderly is still an evolving project, and there are currently no official job openings. If you are interested in getting involved or simply wish to have a discussion, please contact us.
